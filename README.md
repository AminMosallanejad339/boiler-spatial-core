# Industrial Water-Tube Boiler — Spatial Core

An interactive 3D knowledge map covering **12 design groups · 74 chapters · ~140 engineering equations** for industrial water-tube boiler design.

 **Live demo:** https://aminmosallanejad339.github.io/boiler-spatial-core/

## What's inside

- Heat duty & fuel sizing
- Combustion air & FD fan / duct losses
- Furnace radiation (Hottel model)
- Convection & tube-metal temperature
- Design pressure & superheater
- Drums, bank tubes & headers (ASME PG-27 / PG-52)
- Economizer (ESCOA correlation, Grimson draft)
- Circulation ratio (CR test)
- Stack draft, FD fan BHP & PSV sizing
- Desuperheater & efficiency (HHV/LHV)
- Weights, water holdup & component mass
- Reports & layouts

## Stack

Three.js r160 · KaTeX 0.16 · CSS2DRenderer · UnrealBloom · custom GLSL shaders

##  Primary source & attribution

The equation set, chapter organization, and engineering methodology implemented
in this visualization are adapted from the following textbook:

> **Mousapoor, M.** (2022). *Industrial Water Tube Boiler Design [Formulas in Practice]*.
> Berlin / Boston: Walter de Gruyter GmbH.
> ISBN 978-3-11-075661-6 · e-ISBN (PDF) 978-3-11-075708-8 ·
> e-ISBN (EPUB) 978-3-11-075713-2.

All formulas were **independently re-implemented** as code and rendered as
interactive LaTeX; no figures, diagrams, or screenshots from the book are
reproduced here. Chapter groupings and section naming are used for
navigational purposes only.

Additional engineering standards referenced:

- **ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code** — Section I (Power Boilers), Section VIII (Pressure Vessels), PG-27, PG-52.4.
- **Hottel, H. C., & Sarofim, A. F.** (1967). *Radiative Transfer*. McGraw-Hill.
- **ESCOA Engineering Manual** — finned-tube convective correlation.

If you use this project, please cite the original book above.
